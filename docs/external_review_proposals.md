# Research Proposals for External Review

## Project: Multi-Fidelity Aeroelastic Analysis of Honeycomb Sandwich Plates

## Context

This project builds a computational pipeline for analyzing honeycomb sandwich
plates under aerodynamic loading using a fast First-Order Shear Deformation
Theory (FSDT) solver, with machine learning correction layers to close the
gap against high-fidelity COMSOL simulations. Four research proposals were
pursued:

- **P1**: Multi-fidelity correction field (frequencies + mode shapes)
- **P2**: Probabilistic inverse damage identification (damage field posterior)
- **P3**: Mode-aware reliability surrogate (flutter boundary prediction)
- **P4**: Continuous stability-margin estimation from transient response

All engineering infrastructure is verified (530/530 tests green). The
scientific results and their limitations are documented in RESEARCH_LOG.md.

## Proposals for Review

We request evaluation of the following 9 research proposals generated by
a 5-angle innovation swarm (first-principles, cross-domain analogy,
contrarian, practitioner, theoretician) and independently scored by 3
judges (impact, novelty, feasibility). We are particularly interested in:

1. Whether the identifiability analysis is mathematically rigorous
2. Whether the NPE/SBI proposal is feasible for our data regime
3. Whether we are missing a fundamentally better approach
4. Which of these would strengthen a journal submission most

### Proposal FP-1: Exact Adjoint Identifiability Atlas

**Motivation**: Damage enters the stiffness matrix affinely:
K(f) = Σ_c f_c K_c, where f_c is the retention factor per quadrature cell.
The Hellmann–Feynman theorem gives exact frequency sensitivities:
∂ω_k/∂f_c = φ_k^T K_c φ_k / (2ω_k). From these, the whitened Fisher
information singular spectrum answers — with no modeling assumptions:
how many independent damage directions the measurement channels resolve,
where the blind cells are, what latent dimension the decoder should target.

**Implementation**: Refactor the per-cell quadrature assembly to expose
per-cell stiffness matrices. Compute per-mode sensitivities. Whiten by
channel noise. SVD → effective rank, nullspace, blind-spot map, D-optimal
sensor placement.

**Expected outcome**: The identifiable dimensionality k* of the inverse
problem. If k* ≪ 64 cells, the roadmap pivots from richer posteriors to
measurement-channel enrichment. Preliminary FD analysis suggests k* ≈ 3–6.

**Effort**: Days. Purely additive on existing code.

### Proposal FP-2: Mechanism-Resolving Damage Parametrization + Structured Likelihood

**Motivation**: Two approximations are physically false:
(i) Isotropy — one scalar retention scales ALL stiffness blocks uniformly,
but real mechanisms separate (matrix cracking → D, debonding → B,
core crush → As). (ii) Likelihood — pixel-independent Gaussian noise,
but residuals are spatially correlated systematics (~16× understatement
of joint spread, raw SBC p ≈ 1e-50, rescued only by conformal patching).

**Implementation**: (a) Per-channel retention d(x,y) = [d_A, d_B, d_D, d_As]
with mechanism-specific generators (crack → anisotropic D; debond →
antisymmetric B; core crush → As/A). (b) Low-rank factor-analysis likelihood
replacing pixel-iid NLL. Success gate: raw SBC p > 0.05 WITHOUT conformal.

**Expected outcome**: Honest generative class; raw uncertainties that pass
calibration on their own merits.

**Effort**: Medium. Dataset regeneration + retraining required.

### Proposal C-1: Identifiability Audit — Likelihood Constrains ~3 of 64 Dimensions

**Motivation**: Independent finite-difference Jacobian analysis shows the
6×64 frequency sensitivity matrix has effective rank ~3 (participation
ratio 1.23). D4 symmetry makes mirrored/rotated patches frequency-identical
to 6e-11 (8-fold ambiguity). Modes 5–6 carry 14–22% basis-truncation bias.
`inject_measurement_noise` has ZERO production call sites — all gates
passed on noiseless data.

**Implementation**: Per-cell stiffness contribution matrices K_c from the
existing quadrature assembly. Fisher information per design → CR maps.
POD re-parametrization of the decoder output in the top-r sensitivity basis.

**Expected outcome**: Definitive statement of intrinsic resolution;
principled replacement of the 'full-field damage map' claim with
identifiable low-dimensional descriptors.

**Effort**: Days. numpy-level work on existing infrastructure.

### Proposal C-2: Misspecification Audit — Break the Circularity

**Motivation**: All SP3/SP4 gates passed on noiseless shapes and frequencies
generated AND judged by the same truncated operator (inject_measurement_noise
has zero production call sites). The forward-model error and the estimator
error are entangled. An independent audit separating these two error sources
is prerequisite for any meaningful comparison.

**Implementation**: (a) Evaluate the trained model on frequencies from an
independent FE reference (or at minimum, from a different basis order).
(b) Quantify the forward-model truncation error separately from the
estimator error. (c) Report gates with and without the forward-model bias.

**Expected outcome**: Honest decomposition of total error into
forward-model bias + estimator variance + noise.

**Effort**: Days. Mostly evaluation protocol.

### Proposal T-1: Function-Space Bayesian Inversion via Neural Posterior Estimation

**Motivation**: The CVAE+conformal stack exhibits canonical posterior
collapse (ELBO pinned at the free-bits floor, bistable init-std geometry,
KL annealing provably inert). This is the signature of forcing an amortized
Bayesian inverse problem through a reconstruction-ELBO VAE. Neural
Posterior Estimation (NPE) trains q(d|y) directly on (measurement, field)
pairs — no ELBO, no collapse, SBC is the native diagnostic.

**Implementation**: Replace train_field_cvae_heteroscedastic with an
NSF/MAF conditional density estimator over the low-dim PCA space of
retention grids, conditioned on the ModeShapeCNNEncoder embedding.
Validate with the UNCHANGED evaluate_sp_gates and held-out audit harness.
The deployed CVAE+conformal stack becomes the ablation baseline.

**Expected outcome**: SP3/SP4 passing without sbc_pvalue_uncalibrated ≈
1e-50; genuine epistemic/aleatoric decomposition; paper-grade story:
"sandwich-plate NDT as amortized function-space Bayesian inversion."

**Effort**: Medium-high. CPU-only training is the main schedule risk.

### Proposal A-1: Ensemble Kalman Inversion

**Motivation**: Weather prediction abandoned direct learning for EnKF.
EKI is physics-in-the-loop (each iteration runs the forward solver),
bypasses posterior collapse by construction, and handles the spatial
correlation through the ensemble covariance.

**Implementation**: ~100 lines wrapping the existing batched forward model.
Ensemble size ~100. Inflation tuning on 64-dim fields is the main exposure.

**Expected outcome**: Collapse-free inversion; clean CVAE-vs-EKI comparison.

**Effort**: Medium. CPU-friendly.

---

### Proposal FP-3: Von Kármán Nonlinearity, LCO Margins, Breathing-Damage Signatures

**Motivation**: Linear FSDT + piston theory produce transients that are
pure sums of exponentials growing without bound. Real panels survive
supercritical flight in finite-amplitude limit-cycle oscillations.
Breathing cracks have vanishing linear signature — the accessible channel
is nonlinear vibro-acoustic modulation.

**Implementation**: Von Kármán modal coupling coefficients + time
integration at n_m≈20 mode truncation. LCO amplitude via harmonic
balance. Breathing-stiffness measurement model for harmonic/sideband
features.

**Expected outcome**: Quantified nonlinear validity horizon; LCO amplitude
margins; breathing-crack detection channel.

**Effort**: High. Months. New solver class required.

---

### Proposal A-2: Sequential Bayesian Monitoring (SHM pattern)

**Motivation**: Bridge SHM uses sequential Bayesian updating. Apply to P2:
update the damage posterior as new measurements arrive, rather than
one-shot prediction.

**Implementation**: Particle filter or sequential MC over the field
posterior, updated per new measurement.

**Effort**: Medium. Standard method adaptation.

## Questions for the Reviewer

1. Is the identifiability analysis (FP-1 / C-1) mathematically rigorous?
   The Hellmann-Feynman identity requires non-degenerate eigenvalues; the
   D4 symmetry of the square plate creates degenerate pairs. How should
   degenerate-pair sensitivity be handled?

2. Is NPE/SBI (T-1) feasible for 64-dimensional fields with only 1000
   training samples on CPU? The normalizing flow would need to model
   a 64-dim distribution conditioned on a ~64-dim embedding.

3. Are we missing a fundamentally better approach? The CVAE posterior
   collapse, the conformal dependency, and the ~3-dimensional effective
   rank all suggest a mismatch between the model class and the problem.

4. Which of these proposals would strengthen a journal submission most?
   We are targeting [Journal of Sound and Vibration / Mechanical Systems
   and Signal Processing / Structural Health Monitoring].
